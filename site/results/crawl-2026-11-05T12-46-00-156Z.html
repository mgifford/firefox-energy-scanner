What these numbers mean

This report contains three different classes of sustainability indicator. They have different system boundaries and different units, and they are not interchangeable. The first two are always present. The third appears only when a grid intensity was declared.

A. Modelled CO2e (CO2.js)
Estimated emissions derived from transferred bytes and model assumptions. Not a measurement.
B. Observed client energy (Firefox power counters)
Energy drawn by Firefox processes on this client machine during the workload. Scope is the browser's processes — not the display, not the server, and not the network.
C. Grid-derived carbon (from B, not from A)
Carbon implied by the electricity measured in B, at a grid intensity declared by the operator. It is derived from observed energy and carries that assumption with it. It is not a conversion of the CO2.js estimate, and the CO2.js estimate is never converted into it.

The measurement boundary is the client machine only. These results do not measure server, PHP, database, data-centre, or whole-Internet energy, and they are not a total carbon figure for the site.

Warnings

No power counters were found in the Gecko profile. Energy values are unavailable on this platform; timing and network metrics are still valid.

Crawl mode is for discovery and broad assessment. It is not a controlled regression workload; use journey mode for A/B comparisons.

Methodology

Energy is attributed to a step by time window: the harness records wall-clock boundaries and integrates Firefox's power counters over that range. Any concurrent browser activity inside the window is included. Counter samples are picowatt-hours, converted with joules = pWh × 1e-12 × 3600.

Baseline correction retains all three values — raw measured energy, the estimated idle energy, and the incremental difference. Negative incremental values are reported as-is and flagged as noisy rather than clamped to zero.

Class C is opt-in. It is omitted entirely unless a grid intensity was declared for the session, and it is derived from Class B only — the CO2.js estimate is never converted, summed or averaged with anything. See docs/methodology.md.
