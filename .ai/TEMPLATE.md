# Reflection template

Copy this to `.ai/reflections/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. Keep the whole entry under
150 words. Cite evidence: a command and its output, a test name, a file, or a
measurement. A reflection with no evidence is an opinion.

---

# YYYY-MM-DD — what changed, in one line

**Task:** what was asked, in one sentence.

**Changed:** the files or areas that moved.

**Evidence:** the command that proves it, and what it printed.

**Worked:** what to repeat.

**Cost time:** what to avoid next time.

**Next:** the single next action, and who owns it.
